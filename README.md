## 🇨🇳 中文

这是一条强制 5 步的产品贴字 TVC 管线：先收产品参考图和品牌名，再生成正面、侧面、背面、45 度 Hero 与局部微距的产品多视角图锁死产品身份，然后产出 3×3 分镜九宫格，再按分镜拆成 3–5 段视频逐段确认，最后拼接并配产品品类对应的 BGM。文字始终是空间里的图形装置而不是底部字幕，内置环形文字、折叠拉伸大字、透视纵深文字、被人物或产品遮挡等玩法，并提供产品电影化拆解、品牌世界穿梭、生活方式短片三种植入策略。它把产品出镜率当铁律：全片产品可见格不低于 70%，单张 Grid 无产品格不超过 2，禁止连续 3 格无产品。每一步产物都落到画布，并在进入高成本步骤前停下来等你确认。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 一张产品参考图 → 正面、侧面、背面、45 度 Hero、微距五视角锚定图，先锁产品身份再谈分镜
- 3×3 分镜九格逐格标注镜号、产品出现方式、文字动效、运镜方向、转场逻辑与 BGM 卡点
- 15 秒拆 3–5 段视频，每段用分镜格作镜头锚点、多视角图作产品基准，逐段确认只重做不满意那段
- 文字是空间图形装置：环形文字、折叠拉伸大字、透视纵深文字、被产品遮挡，不做底部字幕条
- 产品出镜铁律：全片产品可见格 ≥70%，单张 Grid 无产品格不超过 2，禁止连续 3 格无产品
- 成片按品类配 BGM：科技数码、运动户外、美妆香氛、食品饮料、汽车机械各有节奏方向，重拍对齐文字弹出与转场

## 🇬🇧 English

This is a forced five-step product typography TVC pipeline: collect a product reference image and brand name, generate multi-view product shots (front, side, back, 45-degree hero, macro detail) to lock product identity, produce a 3x3 storyboard grid, split it into 3-5 video segments confirmed one by one, then concatenate and score with BGM matched to the product category. Typography is always a graphic device in real space, never a bottom subtitle, with built-in moves like ring text, folding and stretching headlines, perspective depth text, and type occluded by people or the product, plus three placement strategies: cinematic product breakdown, brand-world crosscut, and lifestyle film. It enforces product screen time as a hard rule: at least 70% of all cells show the product, no more than 2 product-free cells in any single grid, and never 3 product-free cells in a row. Every step lands on the canvas, and it stops for your confirmation before each high-cost step.

### ✨ What it does

- One product reference image becomes five anchored views (front, side, back, 45-degree hero, macro) before any storyboard work
- A 3x3 storyboard labels every cell with shot number, how the product appears, text motion, camera direction, transition logic, and BGM hit point
- A 15s spot splits into 3-5 segments, each anchored by a storyboard cell and a multi-view product reference, with only the rejected segment redone
- Typography is a spatial graphic device: ring text, folding and stretching headlines, perspective depth text, occlusion by the product, never a subtitle bar
- Hard product rule: at least 70% of cells show the product, at most 2 product-free cells per grid, never 3 in a row
- Final BGM is matched by category (tech, sports, beauty, food and drink, automotive), with beats aligned to text pops and transitions
